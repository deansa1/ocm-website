About OCM

Learning is sacred.

The Orchestral Classroom Model is a whole-school framework that creates, protects and restores the conditions required for effective teaching and learning across the student, teacher and stakeholder experience.

Why OCM exists

Every student has the right to learn free from harassment. Every teacher has the right to teach free from harassment. OCM helps schools make those rights operational through clarity, routines, authority, natural justice, restoration and re-entry.

What makes it different

The classroom remains central, but OCM extends through readiness, transitions, behaviour, leadership consistency, family expectations and evaluation. The framework is designed to be precise enough to teach, practical enough to use and clear enough to test.

Creating, protecting and restoring the conditions for learning — every student, every time.
