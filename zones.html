The Six Zones

The learning purpose determines the zone.

A zone is a protected learning condition, not a room. Teachers select the condition the learning purpose requires and change it only when the intended learning requires a different one.

The model at a glance

The Behaviour Zone protects and restores the conditions around learning. Within it, teachers deliberately establish different learning conditions to suit different purposes.

Gathering

Bring attention, people and materials together so learning can begin deliberately.

Instruction

Explicitly explain, model and scaffold new knowledge, skills and understanding.

Guided Practice

Work with students while checking understanding and providing timely feedback.

Collaboration

Create the conditions for purposeful peer interaction, discussion and shared production.

Independent Practice

Give students space to apply, rehearse and demonstrate learning with appropriate independence.

Reflection & Assessment

Review learning, gather evidence, reflect and determine what should happen next.

Creating, protecting and restoring the conditions for learning — every student, every time.
