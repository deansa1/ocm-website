OCM in Practice

Deliberate conditions, not choreography.

OCM does not prescribe movement for its own sake. It requires movement only when the learning conditions should change, giving the intended outcome the best possible chance of being achieved.

Guided Practice

Students work closely with a teacher while understanding is checked in real time. The condition is deliberately different from explicit instruction or independent practice because the learning purpose is different.

Transitions matter

A transition is successful when the learning condition changes quickly, calmly and predictably without unnecessary loss of time or attention.

Re-entry matters

When behaviour disrupts learning, OCM separates correction and consequence from the later work of restoration and successful re-entry.

Creating, protecting and restoring the conditions for learning — every student, every time.
